# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussion about the work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing someone's private information. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who do this.

To report a problem, use a private [security advisory](https://github.com/vijayksingh/metalui/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct" or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
